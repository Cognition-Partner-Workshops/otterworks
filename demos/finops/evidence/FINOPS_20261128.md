## Local dev proof

Ran the [documented network-isolated Compose workload](../workload/README.md) from the repo root. The 36 GiB candidate leaves 2 GiB above the modeled maximum allocation (27 GiB system reserve plus 7 GiB scratch). Returning to 30 GiB caused HTTP 507s and failed the error, latency and disk-pressure gates; it is not proposed. The app-retention gate checks a 7-day minimum; it does not simulate log expiry.

**Audit policy:** Minimum and configured retention are 90 days. Excluded `/otterworks/dev/audit-service` from the modeled app-retention change and from the savings worksheet. The monitoring Terraform module fixes `audit-service` at 90 days independently of the app setting, and the local workload and Terraform plan keep it at 90 days.

With Terraform 1.9.8, initialized the provider-free comparison module, applied the baseline to disposable local state only, and planned the passing candidate. The plan showed `app_retention_days = 14 -> 7`, `root_volume_gib = 50 -> 36`, and `Plan: 0 to add, 1 to change, 0 to destroy.` The baseline state was removed. No AWS call, CloudWatch group update, volume resize, or shared fleet change was made. The proof does not benchmark a real node, and this evidence does not ship any deployable setting changes.
