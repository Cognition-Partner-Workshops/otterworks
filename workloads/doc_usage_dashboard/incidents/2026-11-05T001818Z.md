`OPS.FAULT_SWITCH REFRESH_DOC_USAGE_DASHBOARD is armed: refresh aborted before LOAD_RAW (incident drill)`.

without restoring the OPERATE/MONITOR grants made only by the versioned migration, preventing
the responder from viewing or rerunning the current task. No failed scheduled runs after the
last success were visible under the required identity at detection; do not infer their IDs or times.

task replacement, before resume, and adds regression coverage. Deployment connections honor
