# BW6 conversion wave plan

Run **tib-d1111**. This is a conditional ordering plan, not approval to convert or release. All 24 processes are classified **needs-human-decision** and remain in the **Held for decision** lane. Every wave is currently empty of releasable processes; its members are only **planned (currently held)** slots.

## Ordering rule

Put shared-resource and property groundwork first. Convert callees and producers before or together with their callers and consumers. The source-derived [graph.json](graph.json) summary has no in-project producer→consumer destination matches, so this ordering is driven by call_process edges and shared resources, not an assumed queue pipeline. See the [dependency graph](dependency-graph.md). Call edges remain unresolved name bindings until D-04 is decided. Any flow blocked by an open decision stays held, even when its proposed wave is reached.

Blocking IDs below are generated from the **Affected processes** lists in [decisions.md](decisions.md), not independently assigned. Every process includes open D-09, D-11, D-23 and D-24, plus its other listed decisions. D-25 scope confirmation also affects every process. Wave-wide entry gates can be stricter than a process’s own blocker list.
