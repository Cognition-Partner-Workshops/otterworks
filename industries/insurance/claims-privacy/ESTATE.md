## The six PII columns as an examiner sees them

| Column | Source | `claims_reader` sees |
|---|---|---|
| claimant name | FNOL `GivenName` + `Surname` | initials, rest `*` |
| tax id | FNOL `TaxId` | `XXX-XX-` + last four |
| date of birth | synthetic | `XXXX-XX-XX` |
| phone | FNOL `Phone` (6 of 16 FNOLs have none) | `XXX-XXX-` + last four |
| address | synthetic street + FNOL loss city, state, postal code | `[redacted]`, city, state, postal code |
| bank account | synthetic | last four only |

Synthetic values are generated deterministically from the claim number and
are listed per row in `claims.claimant.synthetic_fields`. Everything else
comes from the corpus exactly as written.

## Retention policies

| Policy name | Rule | Default | Procedure |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rate-audit-7y` | RA-1 | 7 years after the later of `effective_to` and the last ledger period; never while open | `rate_retention_pkg.purge` |
| `claims-pii-closed` | CP-1 | `retention_years` (7) after the later of `closed_at` and the last payment value date; skips legal hold and reopened | `claims.purge_closed_claim_pii` |

Stores outside both purges are listed in [RETENTION-POLICY.md](RETENTION-POLICY.md#outside-these-procedures).

## Corpus

16 FNOL, 14 payment and 10 assignment messages, 40 in all. Every message is
loaded exactly once; [seed/RECONCILIATION.md](seed/RECONCILIATION.md) has one
row per message.
